# 🚀 Launch Copy: Taekwondo Tournament Management System

## 🎯 Product Hunt Launch

**Name:** Nexus Tournament Manager (or Taekwondo Tournament Manager)
**Tagline:** Real-time martial arts tournament orchestration without the Excel chaos.

**Description:**
Running a martial arts tournament is chaotic—paper brackets, delayed matches, and confused competitors. We built the Nexus Tournament Manager to modernize the dojang. 

Say goodbye to manual Excel brackets. Our system automatically parses Excel rosters to generate dynamic sparring and pattern brackets, synchronizes real-time schedules across multiple rings, and drives beautiful, high-contrast digital displays for attendees. 

**✨ Core Features:**
- **Automated Bracket Generation:** Instant single/double-elimination bracket creation from `.xlsm` rosters.
- **Live Scheduling & Sync:** Keep multiple rings/mats synchronized in real-time.
- **Dedicated Dashboards:** Tailored interfaces for Tournament Directors and Scorekeepers.
- **Digital TV Displays:** High-contrast live leaderboards and schedules for the crowd.
- **Public Registration:** Self-service entry for competitors.

Built as part of the Nexus AI Business Solutions Portfolio, this is step one towards a fully white-labeled SaaS for martial arts studios everywhere. 

We’d love your feedback! Let us know what you think below. 👇
