# Bowin managed-pilot offer

**Draft for customer discovery—not a published price list or binding offer.**

## Ideal first customer

An independent Taekwondo school running a local, non-sanctioned or organizer-governed event with 50–300 competitors and 1–6 rings. The organizer must name one decision-maker, approve the configured rules, obtain participant/guardian consent, and participate in a rehearsal.

## Proposed offer to validate

| Item | Founding pilot |
|---|---|
| Suggested price | CAD 249–499 per event, quoted manually |
| Included | Configuration workshop, roster template/import assistance, staging rehearsal, event setup, operator training, launch checklist, event-day escalation coverage, results export, and retrospective |
| Customer provides | Approved rules, lawful participant data and consent, venue devices/network/power, staff, emergency procedures, payment collection, refunds, insurance, and sport/safety decisions |
| Not included | Registration-fee processing, federation certification, medical or legal advice, guaranteed internet access, hardware rental, livestreaming, native apps, athlete rankings, or unattended 24/7 support |
| Cancellation/refund | Must be written into each signed pilot order; no default is promised by this draft |

Pricing is a hypothesis. Record the organizer's current process cost, competitor count, willingness to pay, objections, and support burden after every discovery call and pilot. Do not build subscription billing until at least three qualified organizers accept a paid or explicitly discounted pilot offer.

## Success measures

- Setup is completed without engineering access to production data.
- The director approves every division and rules configuration before brackets are generated.
- Check-in, scoring, advancement, corrections, displays, and results complete without an unrecoverable state incident.
- Operators can perform the critical workflow after training without developer intervention.
- The organizer reports measurable time or error reduction versus the prior workflow.
- Support time and infrastructure cost leave a credible positive contribution margin.

## Sales boundaries

Use “managed Taekwondo tournament operations” and “connection-loss-safe queued scoring/check-in.” Do not say “fully offline,” “all martial arts,” “federation approved,” “guaranteed,” “compliant,” or “secure” without a specific qualified statement and current evidence.
