# Getting Started with Bowin Tournament Manager

Welcome to Bowin! This guide will walk you through setting up your first tournament.

## Quick Start (5 minutes)

### 1. Create Your Organization

After signing in, create your organization from the dashboard. Your organization is your workspace for managing tournaments and team members.

### 2. Import Competitors

Go to **Competitors** → **Import from Excel** to bulk-upload your roster. Download the template, fill in competitor details (name, age, belt, weight), and import.

**Tip:** Keep a master competitor list to reuse across tournaments!

### 3. Create a Tournament

Click **Tournaments** → **New Tournament**. Fill in:
- Tournament name and date
- Location
- Sport type (Taekwondo, Karate, etc.)

### 4. Register Competitors

From your tournament page, go to **Registrations** and add competitors. Select which events they'll compete in (patterns, sparring, or both).

### 5. Auto-Generate Divisions

Navigate to **Divisions** → **Auto-Generate**. The system will:
- Group competitors by age, belt, weight, and gender
- Create fair, balanced divisions
- Show you a preview before applying

### 6. Generate Brackets

Once divisions are finalized, click **Generate Brackets** for each division. The system creates double-elimination brackets with smart seeding to avoid school matchups in early rounds.

### 7. Day-of Operations

On tournament day:
- **Check-In**: Verify competitors and record weigh-ins
- **Scorekeeper**: Record match results in real-time
- **Director Dashboard**: Monitor progress across all rings
- **Public Scoreboard**: Display live results for parents and spectators
