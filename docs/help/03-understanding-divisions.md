# Understanding Divisions

Learn how divisions are created and how to customize them.

## What Are Divisions?

Divisions group competitors with similar characteristics for fair competition:
- **Age**: Grouped into age bands (e.g., 6-7, 8-9, 10-11)
- **Gender**: Male and female divisions
- **Belt Level**: Color belts (CB) vs. black belts (BB)
- **Event Type**: Patterns (forms) vs. Sparring (fighting)
- **Weight Class** (sparring only): Feather, Light, Middle, Heavy

## Auto-Generate Divisions

The system can automatically create divisions based on your registered competitors.

### How It Works

1. Navigate to **Divisions** → **Auto-Generate**
2. Review the preview showing proposed divisions
3. The system will:
   - Split or merge divisions to reach target sizes (8-12 competitors ideal)
   - Avoid divisions with only 1-2 competitors
   - Create separate patterns and sparring divisions
   - Apply weight classes to sparring divisions

### Smart Features

- **School Diversity**: Seeds competitors to avoid early school-vs-school matchups
- **Skill Balancing**: Distributes experienced competitors across divisions
- **Flexible Grouping**: Automatically adjusts age ranges to fill divisions

## Manual Adjustments

After auto-generation, you can:
- **Move competitors** between divisions
- **Split divisions** that are too large
- **Merge divisions** that are too small
- **Rename divisions** for clarity

### Moving Competitors

From the division detail page:
1. Find the competitor you want to move
2. Click **Move to Division**
3. Select the target division
4. Confirm the move

**Note:** Moving a competitor after brackets are generated will require regenerating brackets for both divisions.

## Division Naming

Divisions are automatically named based on their criteria:
- Age range: "10-11"
- Gender: "Males" or "Females"
- Belt level: "CB" (Color Belts) or "BB" (Black Belts)
- Event: "Patterns" or "Sparring"
- Weight: "Lightweight" (for sparring)

Example: "10-11 CB Males Sparring Lightweight"

## Best Practices

- **Generate early**: Create divisions well before tournament day to allow time for adjustments
- **Check balance**: Aim for 4-16 competitors per division (8-12 ideal)
- **Communicate changes**: Notify competitors if you move them to different divisions
- **Lock before brackets**: Finalize divisions before generating brackets to avoid rework

## Troubleshooting

**"Division too small" warning**: Merge with a similar division or allow 2-3 person divisions for special cases

**"Uneven skill distribution"**: Manually adjust seeding in the bracket editor after generation

**"Competitor in wrong division"**: Move them manually before generating brackets
