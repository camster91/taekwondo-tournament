# Team Management

Learn how to add and manage team members in your organization.

## Organization Roles

### Admin
- Full access to everything
- Manage team members
- Organization settings
- Billing management
- Create/delete tournaments

### Director
- Create and manage tournaments
- Import competitors
- Generate divisions and brackets
- Day-of operations
- Results and exports
- **Cannot**: Manage team or billing

### Scorekeeper
- Record match results
- Undo recent scores (5-min window)
- View brackets and schedules
- Check-in competitors
- **Cannot**: Generate brackets, change settings

### Viewer
- Read-only access
- View tournaments and results
- Access public scoreboards
- Export results
- **Cannot**: Make any changes

## Inviting Team Members

### Send Invitation

1. Go to **Organization Settings** → **Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter:
   - Email address
   - First and last name
   - Role (Director, Scorekeeper, or Viewer)
4. Click **Send Invitation**

The invitee receives an email with:
- Magic link to create account
- Their assigned role
- Organization name
- Getting started instructions

### Invitation Status

Track invitations in the team list:
- **Pending**: Invitation sent, not yet accepted
- **Accepted**: Team member active
- **Expired**: Invitation expired (72 hours), resend if needed

### Resending Invitations

If invitation expires or email is lost:
1. Find invitation in team list
2. Click **Resend**
3. New magic link email is sent

## Managing Team Members

### Changing Roles

1. Go to **Organization Settings** → **Team**
2. Find the team member
3. Click **Change Role**
4. Select new role
5. Confirm change

**Note**: Role changes take effect immediately. Active sessions will require re-login.

### Deactivating Members

Temporarily disable access without removing:
1. Find team member
2. Click **Deactivate**
3. Confirm action

Deactivated members:
- Cannot sign in
- Lose all access
- Remain in team list
- Can be reactivated anytime

### Removing Members

Permanently remove from organization:
1. Find team member
2. Click **Remove from Organization**
3. Confirm removal

**Caution**: This cannot be undone. Member must be re-invited to rejoin.

## Tournament-Specific Access

### Granting Tournament Access

For multi-tournament organizations, grant specific access:
1. Open tournament settings
2. Go to **Access Control**
3. Click **Add Team Member**
4. Select member and role for this tournament only

### Use Cases

- Guest scorekeepers for one event
- Temporary helpers
- School-specific directors
- Contractors/vendors

## Best Practices

### Role Assignment Strategy

- **Small organizations (1-2 tournaments/year)**: Most team members are Directors
- **Medium organizations (3-10 tournaments/year)**: Mix of Directors and Scorekeepers
- **Large organizations (10+ tournaments/year)**: Structured hierarchy with specialized roles

### Security

- **Limit Admins**: Only 1-2 trusted admins
- **Review regularly**: Quarterly audit of active members
- **Remove promptly**: Deactivate departing members immediately
- **Unique accounts**: Never share login credentials

### Communication

- **Onboarding**: Send getting started guide to new members
- **Training**: Walk through role-specific tasks before tournament day
- **Expectations**: Clarify responsibilities and access levels
- **Support**: Provide direct contact for technical issues

## Team Coordination

### Pre-Tournament

- Assign roles (who scores which rings, who manages check-in)
- Test access and permissions
- Share tournament details and schedule
- Confirm device/connectivity setup

### During Tournament

- Clear communication channels (group chat, radio)
- Backup personnel for each role
- Director monitors all rings
- Scorekeepers focus on assigned divisions

### Post-Tournament

- Collect feedback from team
- Review what worked/didn't work
- Update procedures for next event
- Thank team members

## Troubleshooting

**"Team member can't sign in"**: Verify email address, check if account is active

**"Wrong permissions"**: Review role assignment, may need to update

**"Invitation not received"**: Check spam folder, resend invitation

**"Need to change role mid-tournament"**: Contact admin; changes take effect immediately

## Multi-Organization Support

If a team member works with multiple organizations:
- They use the same email/account
- Role is per-organization
- Switch organizations via dashboard dropdown
- Access is isolated per organization
