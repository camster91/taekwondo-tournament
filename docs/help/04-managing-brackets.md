# Managing Brackets

Learn how to generate and manage tournament brackets.

## Bracket Types

Bowin supports multiple bracket formats:

### Double Elimination (Default)
- Competitors get a second chance after one loss
- Winners bracket and losers bracket
- Grand finals between winners bracket champion and losers bracket champion
- Best for 4-16 competitors

### Single Elimination
- One loss and you're out
- Fastest format
- Best for 17+ competitors or time constraints

### Round Robin
- Everyone competes against everyone
- Most matches, longest format
- Best for 3-5 competitors
- Ensures maximum participation

## Generating Brackets

### Step 1: Finalize Divisions

Before generating brackets:
- ✅ All competitors are checked in
- ✅ Final weights are recorded (for sparring)
- ✅ Divisions are balanced and finalized

### Step 2: Generate

1. Go to the division detail page
2. Click **Generate Bracket**
3. Choose your bracket format
4. Review the seeding preview
5. Click **Confirm**

### Smart Seeding

The system automatically seeds competitors to:
- **Avoid school matchups** in early rounds
- **Balance skill levels** across the bracket
- **Distribute experience** evenly

## Editing Brackets

### Swapping Competitors

If you need to adjust seeding:
1. Open the bracket editor
2. Click **Swap** on any match
3. Select the two competitors to swap
4. Confirm the swap

**Note:** Only swap before matches begin. Swapping mid-tournament requires regenerating the bracket.

### Bye Matches

For divisions with odd numbers:
- First round will have bye matches
- Byes are distributed fairly based on seeding
- Competitors with byes advance automatically

## Bracket Progression

### Recording Results

See [Day-of Operations](/help/day-of-operations.md) for detailed scoring instructions.

### Advancement Rules

- **Winners bracket**: Winners advance to the next round
- **Losers bracket**: Losers from winners bracket drop down
- **Elimination**: Second loss = elimination
- **Grand finals**: May require two matches if losers champion wins first match

## Printing Brackets

1. Navigate to the division page
2. Click **Download PDF**
3. Print and post at your tournament venue

The PDF includes:
- Complete bracket structure
- Match numbers
- Ring assignments
- Space for manual scoring (backup)

## Troubleshooting

**"Cannot generate bracket"**: Ensure division has at least 2 competitors

**"Bracket progression error"**: Contact support if automatic advancement fails

**"Seeding seems wrong"**: Use the swap feature to manually adjust seeds before the tournament starts
