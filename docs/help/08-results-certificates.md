# Results & Certificates

Learn how to export results and generate certificates.

## Viewing Results

### Tournament Results Page

Navigate to **Results** from the tournament menu to see:
- All divisions and placements
- Match history
- Final brackets
- Statistics and summaries

### Filtering Results

- Filter by event type (patterns/sparring)
- Filter by division name
- Filter by school
- Search by competitor name

## Exporting Results

### CSV Export

Download results as CSV for:
- Spreadsheet analysis
- School reports
- Historical records
- Custom formatting

**Fields included**:
- Competitor name, school, belt
- Division name
- Placement (1st, 2nd, 3rd)
- Matches won/lost
- Event type

### PDF Export

Generate professional PDF reports:
- Complete bracket diagrams
- Final placements
- Match scores
- Formatted for printing

### Excel Export

Full tournament data export including:
- All competitors and registrations
- Division assignments
- Match results and scores
- Timestamps and audit trail

## Generating Certificates

### Individual Certificates

1. Go to **Results**
2. Find the division
3. Click **Generate Certificates**
4. Download PDF with 1st/2nd/3rd place certificates

**Certificate includes**:
- Competitor name
- Division name
- Placement
- Tournament name and date
- Organization logo (if uploaded)

### Bulk Certificates

Generate all certificates at once:
1. Go to **Results** → **Bulk Actions**
2. Select **Generate All Certificates**
3. Download ZIP file with all PDFs
4. Print and distribute at awards ceremony

### Customizing Certificates

In tournament settings, configure:
- Certificate template style
- Signature lines
- Organization logo
- Custom text/messages

## School Reports

### Generating School Reports

1. Navigate to **Results** → **School Reports**
2. Select school from dropdown
3. Download PDF or CSV

**Report includes**:
- School name and summary
- All competitors from that school
- Placements and medals won
- Win/loss records
- Division participation

### Email School Reports

Automatically email reports to school contacts:
1. Ensure school email is in system
2. Click **Email Reports**
3. Select schools to notify
4. System sends PDF report to each

## Publishing Results

### Public Results Page

Share results with:
- Parents and families
- Participating schools
- Tournament sponsors
- Social media

**What's included**:
- Final placements only (1st/2nd/3rd)
- No match-by-match scores (privacy)
- Searchable by competitor name
- Filterable by division

### Social Media Sharing

Export formatted graphics:
- Top 3 per division
- School medal counts
- Tournament highlights
- Ready for Facebook/Instagram

## Historical Records

### Competitor History

View each competitor's past performance:
- All tournaments participated
- Placements achieved
- Win/loss record
- Skill progression over time

### Tournament Archive

Access past tournament results:
- Preserved indefinitely
- Searchable by date/name/school
- Exportable at any time
- Audit trail maintained

## Best Practices

- **Export early**: Download results immediately after tournament
- **Verify accuracy**: Review placements before generating certificates
- **Print extras**: Make extra certificates for no-shows who place
- **Archive locally**: Keep offline copies of all exports
- **Share promptly**: Post results within 24 hours of completion

## Troubleshooting

**"Results missing"**: Verify division brackets were completed

**"Certificate blank"**: Check organization logo and tournament name are set

**"School report empty"**: Verify school name matches registration records exactly

**"Export hangs"**: Large tournaments may take 30-60 seconds; wait before retrying
