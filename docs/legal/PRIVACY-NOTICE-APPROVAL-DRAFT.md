## 1. Scope

Bowin is tournament-management software for martial-arts event organizers. This notice explains how the legal operator of Bowin ("Bowin", "we", "us") handles personal information through the Bowin website, registration tools, organizer workspace, support tools, and tournament operations features.

An organizer that uses Bowin remains responsible for its event, eligibility rules, waivers, safety decisions, public-results policy, and the information it directs Bowin to handle. The signed agreement with an organizer determines the parties' privacy roles for that event.

## 2. Information we handle

Depending on how Bowin is used, we may handle:

- Organizer and staff details, including name, email address, role, organization membership, access status, and service activity.
- Competitor and registration details, including name, date of birth, gender category, belt or rank, school or dojang, region, event selections, check-in status, tournament history, and registration confirmation details.
- Competition-operational details, including divisions, brackets, schedules, match scores, results, seeding, and audit history.
- Parent or guardian details supplied for a minor, including name, email address, phone number, and guardian attestation.
- Event-specific details that an organizer chooses to collect, such as height, weight, accommodation notes, or incident information. Organizers should collect only the minimum information necessary for a legitimate event purpose and should not use free-text fields for medical diagnoses.
- Support details, including the contact name, email address, message, page context, support-ticket status, and correspondence needed to respond.
- Security and service details, including authentication and registration-management tokens, timestamps, rate-limit activity, logs, diagnostics, and security records.

## 3. Why we use information

We use personal information to:

- Provide, secure, and operate the Bowin service.
- Create and manage registrations, confirmations, divisions, brackets, schedules, scores, and results.
- Verify access, prevent abuse, investigate incidents, and maintain service reliability.
- Communicate about registrations, service activity, support, and material service changes.
- Respond to support questions and create or manage support tickets when assistance is requested or an issue needs escalation.
- Meet applicable legal, accounting, safety, contractual, and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell participant personal information or operate an advertising network using participant data. We do not use participant information for unrelated marketing without a separate lawful basis and, where required, a separate choice.

## 4. AI-assisted support

Bowin can be configured with an AI-assisted support provider. When this feature is enabled, the support message and the minimum context needed to answer it may be sent to the configured provider to generate a response or help classify an issue. The assistant may also create or escalate a support ticket when requested or when the message indicates a service issue.

The configured provider, processing location, contract terms, and data-use controls must be listed in the approved subprocessor schedule before this notice is published. Do not enter passwords, payment-card data, government identifiers, medical diagnoses, or other unnecessary sensitive information into support messages.

## 5. Children and guardians

Bowin is not designed for unsupervised child accounts. When an organizer registers a minor, Bowin requires a parent or guardian to attest that they have authority to register that minor. The organizer is responsible for obtaining any additional guardian authorization, waiver, or separate consent required for its event and jurisdiction.

We will present privacy information in a clear and accessible way and will use guardian authorization where meaningful consent cannot reasonably be obtained from the participant. Public display of a minor's name, school, schedule, bracket, or result must be decided by the organizer under its approved event policy.

## 6. Who receives information

We may disclose personal information only as needed to provide and secure Bowin, including to:

- The relevant tournament organizer and its authorized staff.
- Service providers that host the application, provide database or backup storage, deliver transactional email, support security and diagnostics, or provide approved AI-assisted support.
- Professional advisers, insurers, authorities, or other parties where required or permitted by applicable law.
- A successor organization in connection with a merger, financing, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business, subject to applicable law.

Before publication, Bowin will publish a verified subprocessor schedule identifying each provider, purpose, processing location, relevant data categories, and applicable contractual safeguards.

## 7. Public tournament information

An organizer may choose to publish tournament information, such as schedules, brackets, or results, through a public event or scoreboard link. The organizer controls whether this feature is enabled and which operational information is displayed. A published link can be disabled or changed, but information copied or captured by others may remain outside Bowin's control.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, the organizer's instructions, and applicable law. The approved retention schedule will specify periods for registrations, event records, guardian contact details, support records, security logs, and backups.

Bowin may use a recoverable deletion period for operational records. Permanent deletion, backup expiry, and any automatic purge are controlled by the approved retention schedule and production configuration. We will not enable an automatic purge policy until the schedule and recovery process have been approved.

## 9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we handle. These measures include access controls, tenant and role authorization, HTTPS, limited-life authentication or registration-management tokens, rate limiting, audit records, and restricted production access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

If we identify a privacy or security incident, we will investigate, take reasonable containment and remediation steps, and provide notices where required by applicable law.
