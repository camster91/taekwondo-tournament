# Bowin pilot agreement and consent checklist

**Counsel/operator drafting checklist—not a contract or waiver.**

## Pilot order

- Legal identities, addresses, contacts, term, event/date/location, competitor/ring envelope, price, taxes, payment date, cancellation and refund terms.
- Exact included onboarding, configuration, rehearsal, event-day support and post-event deliverables.
- Explicit exclusions: payment collection unless added, hardware/network, medical decisions, officials, insurance, rules certification, livestreaming, and uninterrupted availability.
- Organizer obligation to approve rules/divisions, use trained staff, maintain emergency/manual fallback procedures, and report incidents promptly.
- Support hours, escalation contacts, severity definitions, response targets, maintenance/change freeze, and force-majeure/network limitations.
- Confidentiality, intellectual property/license, feedback permission, publicity/customer-name permission, and no implied endorsement.
- Warranty disclaimers, liability allocation/cap, indemnities, insurance, dispute law/venue, termination, suspension, and data return/deletion.
- Data roles, instructions, subprocessors, security schedule, international transfers, breach cooperation, retention, deletion, audits, and guardian requests.

## Registration notice and guardian action

The approved registration experience should capture separately:

1. Acknowledgement of the current privacy notice and data processing needed to administer the event.
2. Guardian authority when registering a minor.
3. Tournament participation waiver/release drafted for the organizer and jurisdiction.
4. Consent or other lawful authorization for sensitive accommodation/incident information where required.
5. Separate optional permission for public display of a minor's identifying information, school, bracket, schedule, result, photos, video, or livestream.
6. Separate optional marketing permission; it must not be bundled with tournament operation.

Evidence should include document version, timestamp, actor/guardian identity, registration and tournament IDs, and the individual choices. A single boolean “consent” field is not enough for defensible versioned evidence.

## Before signature or launch

- [ ] Legal operator and governing jurisdiction selected.
- [ ] Counsel-approved agreement, privacy notice, consent/waiver and retention schedule.
- [ ] Actual subprocessors and processing locations inserted.
- [ ] Support and security contacts tested.
- [ ] Insurance and event-sanction requirements confirmed by organizer.
- [ ] Product behavior updated to capture the approved versions and choices.
- [ ] Customer receives copies of signed terms and consent/registration records.
